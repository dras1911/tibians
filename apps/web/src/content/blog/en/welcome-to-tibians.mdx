# Welcome to Tibians!

We're glad you're here. **Tibians** is a community hub for Tibia players —
a portal that brings together everything you need to make better
decisions on the Char Bazaar, plan your training, or simply browse the
game world.

## What's on the portal?

- **14 calculators** — from exercise weapons, training time, all the way to
  blessings cost and charm damage.
- **Char Bazaar live** — full auction list with vocation, level, world,
  PvP, BattlEye filters and bid history.
- **Character valuation** — algorithm based on skills, quests, charms,
  store items and progression (calibrated after each auction ends).
- **Daily bosses** — boostable bosses from TibiaData + bestiary with HP,
  EXP and loot lists.
- **Worlds, items, spells, outfits, mounts** — full references with
  filters and pagination.
- **Blog** — yes, this section. Patch updates, deep dives into the
  game's economy and tutorials.

## How to get started?

1. **Bazaar** → filter the list for your setup (e.g. *Knight 300+ EU
   < 15k*) and watch bids live (SSE).
2. **Calculators** → click in the sidebar, each has a *share link* — send
   a pre-filled form to a friend.
3. **Reference** → check `/reference/items` when looking for a specific
   item, market price and whether it's in the store.
4. **Bosses** → see who's worth hunting today at `/bosses` — the boosted
   boss has a higher drop rate.
